Last year I wrote a short post highlighting [where I write on the internet](https://chrishannah.me/where-i-write-on-the-internet/). One of those places was a new blog called "Code and Culture", where I explained it was "a place where I can put my more serious writing".

I remember thinking back then, in an ideal world I'd start writing longer pieces quite regularly, and it would be my way of writing more essays. Instead, with a family, and a busy job, that didn't happen.

However, I have now published three pieces on the blog, so I felt it was about time I shared some links here.

- [When Adulthood Actually Arrives](https://codeandculture.uk/when-adulthood-actually-arrives/) - How my perspective changed after becoming a father.
- [The Ownership Effect](https://codeandculture.uk/the-ownership-effect/) - The effect ownership and responsibility has on your actions.
- [The Work Behind the Work](https://codeandculture.uk/the-work-behind-the-work/) - Short piece on quantifying my work after going from an individual contributor to a team lead.
